    MODEL EVIDENCE

    How well does it work?

    Mean Dice on 50 random BraTS 2021 patients held out from ResEnc training (seed 42).

    ModelWTTCET
    8:2 Ensemble0.9490.9590.936
    SwinUNETR*0.9490.9590.936
    ResEnc (ours, unseen)0.8760.8740.859
    • ~8 s per patient · 1.7 GB peak VRAM on an RTX 4060 laptop GPU.
    • Ensemble ≈ SwinUNETR: at 80:20, ResEnc only changes voxels where Swin is 37.5–62.5% sure.
    • *The public SwinUNETR checkpoint was trained on BraTS 2021, so its score on these patients is optimistic.
    • Expert raters typically agree at ~0.85–0.90 WT Dice. Research demo — not for diagnosis.
