SwinUNETR TRANSFORMER

Hierarchical 3D shifted-window self-attention. Excels at global spatial context across scales.

Advantage: Large receptive field for complex infiltrations
Limitation: Higher memory footprint

Advantage: Fast inference, minimal VRAM
Limitation: CNN-constrained spatial dependencies
