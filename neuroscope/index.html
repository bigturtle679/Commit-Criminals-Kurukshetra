BEYOND THE SURFACE

A little perspective.
A world of possibility.

NeuroScope brings a calm, focused interface to the exploration of brain imaging. Built specifically for neuro-oncology research and clinical education, it transforms raw multi-parametric magnetic resonance scans into synchronized multi-planar slices and interactive 3D spatial reconstructions.

CO-REGISTERED SEQUENCES

Four modalities. One holistic perspective.

INPUT SPECIFICATION

NeuroScope synchronizes four standard MRI sequences into an aligned volumetric coordinate space, isolating distinct radiological characteristics of glioblastoma sub-regions.

SEQUENCE 01

T1-Weighted

Provides structural anatomical baseline. High gray-white matter differentiation reveals cortical topology, midline shift, and mass-effect tissue displacement.

Field: t1ANATOMICAL BASE
SEQUENCE 02

T1Gd / T1-CE

Gadolinium contrast-enhanced sequence. Highlights active breakdown of the blood-brain barrier, demarcating the hyper-vascularized enhancing tumor (ET) boundary.

Field: t1cCONTRAST ENHANCED
SEQUENCE 03

T2-Weighted

Water-sensitive sequence. Exposes microstructural tissue breakdown, non-enhancing tumor elements, and overall abnormal hyperintensity margins.

Field: t2FLUID SENSITIVE
SEQUENCE 04

FLAIR

Fluid-Attenuated Inversion Recovery. Suppresses free cerebrospinal fluid signal to isolate peritumoral vasogenic edema and the true whole tumor (WT) extent.

Field: flairEDEMA DELINEATION

A CLEAR PATH FORWARD

From scan to perspective.

PROPOSED WORKFLOW
01 / PREPARE

Start with your scan.

Upload a multi-modal .zip archive or four individual de-identified NIfTI sequences. Header validation verifies spatial dimensions and orientation before execution.

02 / EXPLORE

Find a new viewpoint.

Choose between our 8:2 Ensemble, SwinUNETR transformer, or SegResNet CNN baseline. Inspect results with linked orthogonal views and rotating 3D surface meshes.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Review and reflect.

Compare volumetric estimates against optional ground-truth BraTS expert annotations with Dice scores, sensitivity, and documented spatial observation bookmarks.
